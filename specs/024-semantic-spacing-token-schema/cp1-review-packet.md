The owner-specified Site / Docs / App proposal is:

| Step | Meaning | Site | Docs | App |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| element | adjacent elements inside one unit | 8px | 4px | 4px |
| group | complete units inside one pattern | 24px | 16px | 16px |
| pattern | page sections | 64px | 32px | 32px |

This is 1/3/8 Site baseline units and 1/4/8 Docs/App baseline units. The App
proposal replaces the live contradictory 8/8/32/16
field/group/section/pattern sequence with 4/16/32 and removes the legacy
`section` alias. Confirm the group-step asymmetry or provide a relationship

inside-out metric terms it yields target occupied Button sizes 40/32/32px at a
16px root. Insets and gaps never merge, even when their values coincide.

## Named exceptions and decisions required

CP1 must explicitly decide these items; silence is not acceptance:

1. **Final count:** confirm ten component roles plus the external pattern
   candidate.
2. **Gap asymmetry:** confirm Site 1/3/8 versus Docs/App 1/4/8 baseline units.
3. **App correction:** approve 4/16/32 element/group/pattern and removal of the
   legacy `section` alias.
4. **Section seams:** decide whether Card, Modal and SidePanel deliberately
   compose adjacent surface insets or whether their containers should own one
   group gap. Do not credit both.
5. **Surface breadth:** confirm that major overlays (Modal/SidePanel) and compact
   Card/Tooltip surfaces share one surface purpose, or admit a purpose-based
   major-overlay breaker.
6. **Compact close action:** Modal and SidePanel repeat the same host-authored
   icon-only Button inset override. Choose a Button variant, an earned
   compact-action role, or a bounded owner exception under FR-047a.
7. **Metric authority:** choose both the acceptance bound (`<=0.5px` CSS or a
   device-pixel bound such as `<=1px` at DPR 1) and whether final authority
   belongs at CP1 or the deferred CP2 engine matrix. Chromium loses `1/64px`
   per closed text element because phase padding and closure margin round down
   separately; the 1px regression guard is not acceptance evidence.
8. **SidePanel title closure:** decide whether component text may suppress the
   full body-line closer. Current SidePanel writes `--space-after: 0`, but the
   accepted T004 closure model is independent of that legacy channel.
9. **ColorInput separator:** retain per-edge actual-border subtraction. Its
   one-sided separator must not inherit nominal symmetric border subtraction.
   Its focused `0.032px` occupied-size tolerance sits just above `1/32px` and
   remains tied to this finding; it MUST NOT widen the general acceptance bound.
10. **Density/fit:** density remains a governed host-fit policy, not a public
    spacing role. If Chip or another enrolled child still enlarges its host at
    dense values, stop for a component-fit decision under FR-044a.
11. **TokenSwatch inline gap:** keep `token-swatch-inline-gap` component-owned
    unless CP1 can prove recurrence under the same causal relationship. Current
    numerical similarity alone does not earn a shared role.
12. **Classified exception backlog:** approve the three explicit exception
    classes above as bounded recut work, or name the class/records that must be
    resolved before CP1. In particular, do not infer roles from frozen-row
    membership or legacy alias names alone.
