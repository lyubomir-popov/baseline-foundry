Substitutability

Element ownership makes substitutions predictable: a replacement adopts the spacing of its semantic or explicit BF role instead of inheriting an anonymous wrapper gap.

Semantic substitution

A native heading receives its heading rhythm from the element.

A paragraph promoted to the h3 role receives the same explicit role rhythm.

The replacement contract is visible in the class, not hidden in its parent.

Control substitution

A button, input, and select each preserve their own document-flow boundary.

Swapping the control does not require a container-owned spacing mode.
