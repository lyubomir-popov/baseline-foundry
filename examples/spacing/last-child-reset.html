Scoped boundary compensation

Elements keep their document-flow after-space by default. A purpose-built control row may trim only its direct control margins where the row's own alignment and padding define the visual boundary.

Normal document flow

The final button keeps its element-owned after-space because it may be followed by more document content.

Purpose-built control boundary

Only the button inside this explicit control row has its block compensation trimmed; unrelated descendants remain element-owned.
