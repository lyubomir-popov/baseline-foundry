Body-line rhythm

Provisional opt-in (Spec 026, Phase A). Adding is-body-line-rhythm to a .bf-theme root gives prose-flow paragraphs, headings and list items a phase inset after their measured nudge and a closure to whole body lines. Each comparison shows the current baseline-unit ledger beside the opt-in ledger for the active tier; the ruling marks every body line.
