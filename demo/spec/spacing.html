Spacing

One ownership model governs every built-in tier: containers express semantic relationships, while text retains only the metric adjustments required for baseline alignment.

Ownership

Containers express meaning

Use bf-stack and its density modifiers for vertical relationships. Patterns may own a focused internal gap when their structure has a stable semantic meaning.

Text retains metric geometry

Heading and body roles keep their measured start nudge and complementary end margin. Those values align glyphs; they do not choose the semantic distance to the next sibling.

Composition scale

is-flush
One continuous title or text relationship with no semantic gap.
is-extra-dense
The smallest explicit separation for tightly constrained component internals.
is-dense

Default stack
The standard boundary between complete internal groups.
is-section and is-section-deep
Major document and campaign boundaries, selected deliberately rather than inherited from child roles.

Authoring guidance

  • Wrap semantic siblings in the stack that describes their relationship.
  • Nest stacks when a title pair, content group, and pattern boundary need different densities.
  • Do not add role-specific bottom spacing, last-child resets, negative gaps, or consumer pixel offsets.
  • Review the component or pattern demo that owns a relationship; diagnostic spacing pages are not part of the public catalog.
