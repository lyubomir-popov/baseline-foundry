Tier contract

OS has the same support contract as every other BF tier. Its denser values are intentional product choices, not a reduced-support exception.

  • Metrics-derived nudges stay active.
  • Nested stacks own semantic spacing, matching every built-in tier.
  • Body and heading roles stay on the canonical `body` plus `h1` to `h6` contract.
  • Direct bundles and class-switched surfaces expose equal public tokens.
