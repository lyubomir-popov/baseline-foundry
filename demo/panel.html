What changed

`panel` no longer needs to stand outside the site shell to represent dense control work. OS now lives beside `editorial`, `documentation`, and `app` as a truthful built-in tier, while the `panel` preset remains available as a legacy alias for downstream compatibility.

Tier contract

OS is deliberately not a canonical spec tier. It is a dense addendum for downstream operator and inspector surfaces that still need real metric-derived alignment instead of app-style zero nudges.

  • Metrics-derived nudges stay active.
  • Spacing remains element-owned, not app-style container owned.
  • Body and heading roles stay on the canonical `body` plus `h1` to `h6` contract.
  • The legacy `panel` preset remains as a compatibility alias, not as the primary mental model.
