Editorial spacing

The flow is element-owned and baseline-compensated.

Paragraphs keep their own spacing so content can be substituted without parent wrappers learning new rules or special cases.

That principle matters just as much in a lean design system as it does in a broader framework. The smaller the surface, the less room there is for hidden exceptions.

Baseline alignment stays immutable. The component surface can shrink, but the rhythm contract should not.
