Editorial Pressure

Portfolio and essay-style composition

This page pressure-tests the same Sans default against a future portfolio/editorial use case: hero text, supporting narrative, quote treatment, and a figure-led grid composition.

Field notes

A restrained Sans rhythm can still carry narrative work if the hierarchy stays disciplined.

The future portfolio does not need a sprawling framework surface. It needs a dependable prose system, a clear grid, and enough compositional range to hold essays, projects, and quiet supporting metadata.

Note

Two font sizes per page should remain the norm, not an aspiration we break casually.

The point is not minimalism for its own sake.

The point is to keep the surface lean while making each primitive fully trustworthy. When a heading, list, quote, card, or figure lands on the rhythm without special pleading, the system becomes easier to reuse and much easier to evolve.

That matters just as much for a personal portfolio as it does for a dense production interface. The content changes, but the discipline around baseline, spacing ownership, and compositional clarity should not.

Keep the invariant small and strong. Let the compositions grow out of it.
