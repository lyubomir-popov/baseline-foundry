This second site shell is intentionally longer than the viewport, so its sticky footer follows the real content rather than overlaying it.

It also stresses wrapping at narrow widths with a deliberately descriptive piece of content about linked layout regions, vertical rhythm, and logical inline sizing.

A site layout owns only its flex structure. Paragraph and heading margins remain the semantic spacing owners in every supported tier.

The repeated content makes the difference observable without a global minimum height or an opaque page-specific spacer.

Footer placement therefore remains a direct consequence of the document length and the explicit site shell contract.
